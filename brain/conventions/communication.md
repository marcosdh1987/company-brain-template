# Communication convention

> Where each conversation lives, so agents and people know where to look for
> context and where to leave a record.

- **Decisions:** land in `brain/decisions/` (ADRs), not in chats.
- **Channels:** _PENDING — what goes to Slack/Teams, what to issues, what to docs._
- **Language:** _PENDING — language for code, comments, PRs, docs._
- **Meetings with decisions:** _PENDING — who records the resulting ADR._
